# Gallery notice

`gallery/pairs/` contains copies of public web pages (Wikipedia, gov.uk, usa.gov, CERN, W3C,
CNN Lite, NPR Text, Hacker News, Craigslist, Berkshire Hathaway) rewritten by reskin in the style
of other brands. They are there only to demonstrate what the tool does.

- The page text belongs to its publishers. Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA 4.0; gov.uk text is under the
  Open Government Licence v3.0; other pages are © their publishers. None of it is licensed here.
- "In the style of" means colors, type and spacing are approximated from the brand's public site.
  No logos or brand assets are copied. The brands are not affiliated with this project and do not endorse it.
- Want a page removed? Open an issue and it will be taken down.
