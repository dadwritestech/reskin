Financial-infrastructure calm. White canvas, a pale blue-grey surface for panels, deep
navy ink instead of black, one electric indigo for primary actions and links. Display
headings are light weight (300-400) with negative tracking, large and airy. Cards sit
on white with the signature long, soft, blue-tinted shadow and small radius. Buttons are
tight pills. Numbers want tabular figures. Generous vertical rhythm on 8px steps.
