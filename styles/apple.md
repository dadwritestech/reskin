Product-page restraint. Big confident headlines (semibold, tight tracking), lots of white,
content in rounded parchment-grey tiles (#f5f5f7) with large radii. Body copy 17px with
generous leading. Links are blue, no underline until hover. Primary actions are blue
pills. Hairlines are rare and light; separation comes from tiles and space. Centered
column, nothing crowded.
