Monochrome developer minimalism. Pure white, black ink, grey everything else; colour
only for links and status. Headings very tight (-0.04em) and semibold. Everything framed in
1px light-grey borders with small radii, like dashboard cards; mono for metadata, counts,
timestamps and code. Primary buttons are solid black with white text. Grid feel, crisp.
